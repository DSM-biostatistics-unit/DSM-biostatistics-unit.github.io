## Research

My research interests lie in statistical and machine learning methods for high-dimensional data, with the goal of developing interpretable and well-calibrated prognostic and diagnostic models. My work spans genetic association studies, polygenic risk scores and rare-variant analyses, as well as clustering techniques and deep learning for time-series and signal data such as electronic health records (EHR) and ECG. Working closely with clinicians, I seek to translate these methods into patient stratification and risk prediction tools that support precision medicine.
