## Research

My research lies at the intersection of network science, Bayesian statistics and bioinformatics. I focus on developing principled methods for open problems in genomics and systems biology. I am particularly interested in statistical models for network-structured data, from exponential random graph models to Bayesian latent-field models defined on biological networks. I use them to integrate individual genetic variation with molecular interaction maps and to model it at the level of the single patient. Alongside my doctoral work, I am a biostatistician at an Italian research hospital, where I design and carry out statistical analyses for clinical, biomedical, pharmacological and genetic projects. I am committed to open and reproducible science through the development of R packages.
