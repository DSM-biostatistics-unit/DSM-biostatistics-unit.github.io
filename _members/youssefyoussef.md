## Research


I am a PhD student in Applied Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Trieste, Italy, holding a Master's degree in Computer Science from IIT Roorkee. My research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence, biomedical signal processing, and cardiovascular genetics. Building on my previous work in ECG lead reconstruction using contrastive learning, my current research explores the integration of deep learning-derived cardiac phenotypes from ECG and cardiac imaging with genetic data to investigate the genetic basis of cardiomyopathies and support early disease detection and precision cardiovascular medicine.
