## Research

My work bridges classical biostatistics and data architecture, with a strong focus on building automated, reproducible pipelines for complex clinical datasets like electronic health records (EHR). 
I specialize in data integration and automated reporting to support transparent clinical decision-making. 
Currently, I am collaborating with a cardiology department on a project aimed at improving the secondary prevention of cardiovascular risk in patients undergoing percutaneous coronary interventions.
