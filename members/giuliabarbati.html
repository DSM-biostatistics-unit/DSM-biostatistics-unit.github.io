Research

My methodological work bridges classical statistical frameworks and modern predictive modeling, driven by a commitment to causal inference and model interpretability - particularly through multi-state models for complex time-to-event data and methods for extracting insights from electronic health records (EHR). I am also interested in using machine learning and deep learning as tools for prediction and decision-making. In the last years, I collaborated with pharmaceutical companies to generate real-world evidence through comparative EHR studies and target trial emulation.
